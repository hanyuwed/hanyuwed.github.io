# Spanish course audio

MP3 recordings synthesized with Piper's `es_ES-sharvard-medium` voice (speaker F).
The model card credits the University of Edinburgh's Spanish Harvard corpus
and its CC BY 3.0 dataset license. These course utterances were synthesized
from the HanyuWed text, using the trained voice.

- [Piper voice model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/es/es_ES/sharvard/medium/MODEL_CARD)
- [Spanish Harvard corpus](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/574)
- [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
